## Psalm 23: the Lord is my shepherd

> The Lord is my shepherd; I shall not want. (Psalm 23:1, KJV)

The shepherd image is about **provision**, not control. Sheep are led to green pastures; they are not
dragged. The sermon's question: where am I resisting being led because I would rather steer?

## Matthew 6: consider the lilies

> Consider the lilies of the field, how they grow; they toil not, neither do they spin. (Matthew 6:28, KJV)

Worry borrows tomorrow's trouble and pays for it with today's peace. The practice offered: name one
worry each morning, then name one provision from yesterday next to it.

## Micah 6:8: what is required

> He hath shewed thee, O man, what is good; and what doth the Lord require of thee, but to do justly,
> and to love mercy, and to walk humbly with thy God? (Micah 6:8, KJV)

Three verbs, in order: do, love, walk. Justice is an action, mercy is an affection, humility is a pace.
