## Gratitude on a slow week

Nothing dramatic happened this week, and that is the gift. Three ordinary mercies: a long call with an
old friend, an evening walk without my phone, and finishing a task I had avoided for a month.

## On waiting

Waiting feels like wasted time only when I treat it as the gap between two real things. What if the
waiting *is* the real thing being formed in me? Patience is learned in queues, not in sermons about queues.

## A question to carry this month

Where am I busy in a way that keeps me from being still? Write down one place, and leave one hour of it
empty on purpose.
