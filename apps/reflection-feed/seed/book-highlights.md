## Augustine, Confessions, Book I

> Thou hast made us for Thyself, and our heart is restless, until it repose in Thee.

Restlessness is not a malfunction. It is a compass needle that has not yet settled. (Pusey translation, 1838.)

## Brother Lawrence, The Practice of the Presence of God

> The time of business does not with me differ from the time of prayer.

Washing dishes in the monastery kitchen counted as much as the chapel. The point is not to stop working,
but to stop dividing the day into holy and ordinary hours.

## Thomas à Kempis, The Imitation of Christ

> Be not angry that you cannot make others as you wish them to be, since you cannot make yourself as you
> wish to be.

A useful check before any hard conversation: am I asking of them what I have not managed myself?
