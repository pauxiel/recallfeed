CI therefore runs no separate unit or integration pass. The one extra command (the result check) exists only because Vitest has no "fail on skip" option.
- **Unchanged:** lint rules including the 1,000-line `max-lines` rule, all existing safety checks and tests, and the 80% coverage thresholds. No test was weakened, skipped or removed. No new test framework, orchestration, or version matrix.
